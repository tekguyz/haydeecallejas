## Platform

web

## Users

Women who buy luxury handbags and accessories, in Nicaragua and abroad, who want to see a real designer with a story and then ask for a piece. Haydee herself has the final say on every creative and business choice.

## Product Purpose

The new website for HAYDEE CALLEJAS, a Nicaraguan luxury accessories brand: exotic skins and hand-cut leather, made with master artisans. Two jobs, in this order on every screen: show a real designer with a story, and make "I want that bag, how do I get it" one tap away (WhatsApp).

## Positioning

Handmade limited editions by a designer who has worked with Nicaraguan artisans since 2000: "Arte de Vivir" and "Lo nuestro". No two skins are alike.

## Operating Context

Spanish. Buying is by WhatsApp, not a cart. No prices shown.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Open: whether the 16 catalog pictures are real photos; the showroom city and hours, stockists and piece names Haydee must confirm (issue #158).

## Brand Commitments

- The picked look is binding: `DESIGN.md`, option 1 Portada (2026-11-14). Do not roll a new visual world.
- Never quiet cream with a thin serif: the July 2026 site and two previews were rejected for exactly that. Never a template shop with cut-outs on white tiles.
- Her logo, the H+C monogram, unchanged.
- WhatsApp green only on the WhatsApp icon.

## Evidence on Hand

Her story with sources in the client README (1991 cultural attaché, 2000 artisans, 2016 Bienal Iberoamericana finalist in Madrid). Claims from her old site (USAID 2011) stay marked as hers.

## Product Principles

- Her real pictures carry the page; type and color stay out of their way.
- Every fact has a source; invent nothing.
- One tap from wanting a bag to asking for it.
